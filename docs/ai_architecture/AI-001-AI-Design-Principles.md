# AI Design Principles

> "Consistent intelligence emerges from consistent principles."

## Purpose

Defines the engineering principles that guide every AI capability developed within Ascend.

---

## Philosophy

Every AI component should be modular, explainable, measurable, and designed to augment human decision making rather than replace it.

---

## Core Principles

- Human-centered
- Context-first
- Provider-agnostic
- Deterministic orchestration
- Privacy by design
- Safety by default
- Observable
- Extensible

---

## Design Objectives

Every AI feature should:

- Respect user intent
- Produce consistent behavior
- Minimize hallucinations
- Explain important decisions
- Operate within policy boundaries
- Improve through evaluation

---

## Architectural Guidelines

Build AI systems that are:

- Modular
- Composable
- Testable
- Versioned
- Replaceable

Avoid tightly coupling business logic to specific models.

---

## Quality Standards

Evaluate:

- Accuracy
- Relevance
- Latency
- Cost
- Safety
- User satisfaction

---

## Governance

Require:

- Version control
- Experiment tracking
- Review processes
- Continuous evaluation
- Auditability

---

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Hidden prompts
- Hardcoded provider behavior
- Opaque reasoning chains
- Unmeasured AI quality
- Unsafe autonomous actions

---

## AI Context

AI coding agents should implement all intelligent systems according to these design principles before introducing domain-specific functionality.
