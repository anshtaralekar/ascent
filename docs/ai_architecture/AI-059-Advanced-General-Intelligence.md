# Advanced General Intelligence

> "Generality is not simply knowing more; it is being able to transfer competence across domains."

## Purpose

Defines a long-term architectural research direction for highly general AI capabilities within the conceptual framework of Ascend.

## Philosophy

General intelligence should be evaluated by breadth, transfer, adaptability, reliability, and alignment rather than by a single benchmark or narrow capability.

## Capability Dimensions

Consider:

- Cross-domain reasoning
- Transfer learning
- Long-horizon planning
- Abstract problem solving
- Tool use
- Knowledge integration
- Social and collaborative intelligence
- Adaptation to unfamiliar environments

## General Intelligence Loop

1. Understand objective
2. Acquire relevant knowledge
3. Form a model of the problem
4. Reason across domains
5. Plan
6. Act
7. Evaluate outcomes
8. Adapt
9. Generalize learning

## Architecture Requirements

A highly general system would require strong integration between:

- Reasoning
- Memory
- Knowledge
- Planning
- Agents
- Tools
- Evaluation
- Safety
- Operations

## Evaluation

Assess:

- Novel-task performance
- Transfer ability
- Robustness
- Long-horizon reliability
- Calibration
- Safety
- Human usefulness

Use unfamiliar tasks rather than relying exclusively on memorized benchmarks.

## Autonomy

Greater capability should not automatically imply unrestricted autonomy.

Autonomy must remain bounded by:

- Authorization
- Safety policies
- Resource limits
- Human oversight
- Operational controls

## Research Boundaries

Treat claims of general intelligence as hypotheses requiring empirical evidence.

Distinguish clearly between:

- Demonstrated capability
- Experimental capability
- Research direction
- Speculative capability

## Governance

Require stronger governance as systems gain:

- Broader capabilities
- Greater autonomy
- Persistent memory
- Wider tool access
- Greater ability to affect external systems

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Defining general intelligence through one benchmark
- Equating scale with generality
- Deploying speculative capabilities without evidence
- Removing oversight because a system appears capable

## AI Context

AI coding agents should treat advanced general intelligence as a research architecture built on measurable capabilities, rigorous evaluation, and progressively stronger safety and governance controls.
