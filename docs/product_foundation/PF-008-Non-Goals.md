# Non-Goals

> "Knowing what not to build is as important as knowing what to build."

## Purpose

This document defines the boundaries of Ascend. It prevents feature creep and ensures every decision reinforces the product vision.

---

# Why Non-Goals Matter

Without clear constraints, products gradually become collections of unrelated features.

Ascend intentionally focuses on helping individuals plan, execute, reflect, and grow.

---

# Product Non-Goals

Ascend is **not** intended to become:

- A social media platform
- A team chat application
- An enterprise ERP system
- A CRM
- A document editor competing with office suites
- An entertainment platform

---

# Design Non-Goals

The interface should never prioritize:

- Visual novelty over usability
- Dense dashboards filled with unnecessary information
- Hidden interactions that reduce discoverability
- Gamification without meaningful value

---

# AI Non-Goals

The AI will not:

- Make irreversible decisions on behalf of users
- Hide why recommendations are made
- Encourage addictive engagement
- Replace human judgment

AI exists to assist, not control.

---

# Engineering Non-Goals

Avoid:

- Premature optimization
- Over-engineered architectures
- Vendor lock-in where practical
- Features that significantly increase maintenance without user value

---

# Decision Filter

Before approving any new feature, ask:

1. Does it align with the product vision?
2. Does it support user transformation?
3. Is it better than expanding an existing capability?
4. Will it increase long-term complexity?

If the answer suggests unnecessary scope, the feature should be deferred or rejected.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful product remains:

- Focused
- Cohesive
- Predictable
- Maintainable
- Purpose-driven
