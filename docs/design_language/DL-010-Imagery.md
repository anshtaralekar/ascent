# Imagery

> "Images should communicate authenticity, intention, and possibility."

## Purpose

This document defines the imagery guidelines for Ascend across the product, website, presentations, documentation, and marketing materials.

Imagery should reinforce the brand, not compete with it.

---

# Philosophy

Ascend favors authentic imagery over stock-like perfection.

Images should inspire confidence, focus, and growth while remaining clean and timeless.

---

# Core Principles

## Authentic

Prefer genuine environments, real people, and believable situations.

Avoid artificial-looking compositions.

---

## Purposeful

Every image should support a message.

Questions to ask:

- What does this image teach?
- What emotion should it evoke?
- Does it improve understanding?

---

## Calm

Images should avoid excessive visual noise.

Use restrained compositions with generous negative space.

---

# Photography Style

Preferred characteristics:

- Natural lighting
- Soft shadows
- Neutral backgrounds
- Minimal props
- Real workspaces
- Diverse people

Avoid:

- Overly saturated colors
- Heavy filters
- Artificial HDR effects
- Distracting backgrounds

---

# Product Imagery

Screenshots should:

- Use realistic data
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Reflect current UI
- Avoid placeholder content
- Highlight meaningful workflows

---

# Marketing Visuals

Marketing assets should communicate:

- Growth
- Focus
- Clarity
- Simplicity
- Human achievement

The product should always remain the primary subject.

---

# Backgrounds

Preferred:

- Subtle gradients
- Soft textures
- Neutral surfaces
- Gentle depth

Avoid decorative backgrounds that compete with foreground content.

---

# AI-Generated Images

AI imagery should follow the same visual language as human-created assets.

Requirements:

- Realistic lighting
- Consistent color harmony
- Inclusive representation
- No visual artifacts
- Brand-aligned compositions

---

# Accessibility

Images must:

- Include meaningful alternative text where appropriate
- Maintain adequate contrast with surrounding UI
- Never contain essential information without text equivalents

---

# Engineering Notes

Use responsive image formats, optimized compression, lazy loading, and modern formats where supported.

---

# AI Context

Future AI-generated marketing and product imagery should inherit these guidelines unless a campaign specifies otherwise.
