# Knowledge Base

> "Reliable intelligence depends on reliable knowledge."

## Purpose

Defines the architecture for storing, organizing, validating, and governing structured knowledge within Ascend.

---

## Philosophy

Knowledge should be accurate, attributable, versioned, searchable, and continuously maintained as a trusted foundation for reasoning and planning.

---

## Knowledge Types

- Structured knowledge
- Unstructured documents
- Knowledge graphs
- Reference datasets
- External knowledge sources

---

## Knowledge Lifecycle

1. Ingest
2. Validate
3. Normalize
4. Classify
5. Store
6. Index
7. Retrieve
8. Update
9. Archive

---

## Organization

Structure knowledge using:

- Categories
- Tags
- Relationships
- Metadata
- Version history

---

## Validation

Verify:

- Source credibility
- Data integrity
- Freshness
- Consistency
- Duplicate detection

---

## Versioning

Support:

- Revision history
- Change tracking
- Rollback
- Source attribution

---

## Access Control

Enforce:

- Role-based access
- Tenant isolation
- Document permissions
- Audit logging

---

## Monitoring

Track:

- Knowledge freshness
- Retrieval frequency
- Validation failures
- Storage growth
- Index health

---

## Governance

Require:

- Source attribution
- Policy compliance
- Review workflows
- Retention policies

---

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Anonymous knowledge
- Stale information
- Duplicate repositories
- Missing provenance

---

## AI Context

AI coding agents should treat the knowledge base as the authoritative repository for validated information and preserve provenance throughout ingestion and retrieval.
